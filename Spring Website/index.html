Live Mechanical Calculations

As you adjust physical parameters, the embedded physics engine calculates critical engineering properties instantly.

  • Spring Rate (k): Accurate N/mm or N·mm/deg stiffness ratings.
  • Spring Index (C): Manufacturing feasibility checks with dynamic warnings.
  • Wahl Factor: Stress correction factors for tight-wound coils.
  • Solid Length & Resonance: Operational limits calculated automatically.
  • Material Library: Steel, Stainless, Chrome-Silicon, Titanium, and Bronze parameters built-in.
